# Product

## Register

product

## Users

In-house recruiters and hiring managers at NULogic. They live in this tool for hours a day in bright offices: drafting job descriptions, screening stacks of resumes, prepping interviews, and sending candidate emails. Their job-to-be-done is moving candidates through a pipeline fast without dropping anyone. They are not designers; they value scannability and certainty over spectacle.

## Product Purpose

Recruit360 (NULogic Recruitment AI) automates the slowest parts of hiring: JD generation, resume screening, interview guide creation, and candidate communication, with Gemini doing the heavy reading. Success is a recruiter producing a defensible shortlist in hours instead of weeks, and trusting the scores enough to act on them.

## Brand Personality

Calm precision. Three words: composed, exact, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a sharp analyst's desk, not a sales demo. Data is the hero; chrome recedes. Confidence comes from typography and alignment, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard: identical stat-card grids, icon+heading+text tiles, gradient CTAs, hero metrics with sparkline confetti.
- Heavy enterprise HR software (Workday/SAP energy): dense gray chrome, bureaucratic tables, tiny crowded text.
- AI-product cliches: purple glows, neon gradients, sparkle icons on every AI feature.

## Design Principles

1. The pipeline is the page. Every screen answers "who is in flight and what do they need from me next" before anything else.
2. Scores must be legible at a glance and defensible on click. Numbers get tabular alignment and context, never bare percentages floating in cards.
3. Chrome recedes, content advances. Navigation and containers earn no visual weight beyond what orientation requires.
4. One accent, spent deliberately. NULogic red marks action and alert, never decoration.
5. Motion confirms, never performs. Sub-300ms, compositor-only, always tied to a user action or state change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target (an open audit item tracks known gaps: missing htmlFor, icon buttons without aria-label, modals without focus traps). Light theme is primary: recruiters work in bright offices on laptops. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Color is never the only carrier of meaning on scores and statuses.
